About the project

What is Fast Money Bot?▶

Fast Money Bot (FMB) is a private project created by its author out of a personal interest in algorithmic trading, rather than for commercial purposes.

It serves as a showcase for the author's achievements in algorithmic trading, presenting original developments and their results under real market conditions.

Anyone who shares that interest can take part in testing the trading robot by automatically copying its trades.

How is Fast Money Bot different from other projects?▶

The fundamental difference between Fast Money Bot and conventional commercial services lies in its philosophy:

  • The project continues to exist and develop regardless of how many people join.
  • The author trades primarily for their own benefit.
  • Showing the developments publicly is intended to inform, not to advertise.
  • People interested in joining take the initiative themselves.
  • The author's fee is a by-product of the activity, not the project's main purpose.
How are trades copied?▶

Trades are copied through Markets4you's Share4you system.

The robot trades on the author's own account. The system automatically mirrors those trades on your account based on your trading capital and the copy multiplier you select.

This lets you test the author's algorithm in practice and assess its results yourself.

What are the conditions for joining FMB?▶

You can join freely and there is no subscription fee.

The arrangement is simple: if the author's system generates a positive financial result, the author receives 30% of new profits.

The broker settles payments automatically using a High-Water Mark. Recovering previous trading losses does not trigger another fee.

How much do I need to start trading?▶

The minimum trading deposit is $100.

The author strongly recommends holding additional reserve capital. With the standard copy multiplier of 1, the recommended reserve is 100% of the trading deposit. In exceptionally rare cases, the amount needed may be slightly higher.

If you change the copy multiplier, the recommended reserve changes in direct proportion. For example, with a $100 trading deposit:

  • Multiplier 0.5 — $50 reserve.
  • Multiplier 1 — $100 reserve.
  • Multiplier 2 — $200 reserve.

If reserve capital is already in use on the robot's trading account when you join, the minimum deposit for new participants increases by the amount in use. For example, if $100 in reserves has been added to the robot's $100 base deposit, the minimum for joining during that period is $200.

Holding your own reserve capital is strongly recommended by the author and is optional. You decide whether to set it aside and when to use it.

Where will my money be held, and who manages it?▶

All funds stay in your personal accounts with Markets4you. You do not need to send the author any money.

Through the copy system, the algorithm only carries out trading operations: opening, managing and closing positions.

You remain responsible for managing your capital, choosing your trading exposure, controlling risk and using your reserve funds when needed.

The author has no access to your personal trading accounts or funds.

Do I need to trade on my own?▶

No. The broker copies all trades automatically.

You do not need to analyse the market yourself, open or close trades, install the trading robot or leave a computer running.

How does the FMB trading robot work?▶

The Fast Money Bot trading robot works automatically. It opens, manages and closes positions according to the author's algorithm.

The system combines three trading baskets — trend-following, countertrend and compensation — and has eight proprietary functions.

It trades exclusively in the EUR/USD currency pair.

The algorithm's results are public and you can review them yourself.
