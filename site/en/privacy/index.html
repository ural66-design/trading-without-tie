Privacy policy

General provisions

This Privacy Policy defines how information related to the use of Vladimir1.com, including T-Capital and Fast Money Bot, is processed.

Use of the website

Vladimir1.com does not provide visitor registration, personal accounts or submission of personal data through built-in forms.

The website does not use advertising pixels or third-party visitor behaviour tracking systems.

The selected interface language and progress through the guides are saved directly in the user's browser using local storage.

These settings are not sent to the website's own database and can be deleted by the user through browser tools.

Email

When contacting support@vladimir1.com, the sender's email address, message content and voluntarily provided information may be processed.

The information received is used to consider the enquiry and prepare a reply.

The legal basis for such processing may be the pursuit of legitimate interests relating to the consideration of incoming enquiries, provided the data subject's rights and freedoms are respected.

Correspondence is not published, used for advertising mailings or shared with third-party organisations for marketing purposes.

Email correspondence is received and stored using email infrastructure.

Do not send passport details, passwords, payment details, brokerage account numbers or other confidential financial information to the support address.

For brokerage account and financial transaction matters, contact the relevant broker's support team directly.

Correspondence is kept for the time needed to consider the enquiry, or where other legal grounds for its retention exist.

Technical information

When the website is visited, hosting and technical infrastructure providers may process the IP address, browser and device information, and technical request parameters needed to provide access to the pages.

Vladimir1.com does not use this information to independently create visitors' advertising profiles.

Third-party resources

When following links to third-party resources, information is processed in accordance with the respective services' terms and policies.

Vladimir1.com does not determine how independent third-party organisations process personal data.

Fast Money Bot Telegram bot

Only Telegram user identifiers (Telegram ID) are stored in the Fast Money Bot Telegram bot's own database for its operation.

User names, nicknames, phone numbers and message content are not stored in the bot's own database.

An automated frequently asked questions response system and artificial intelligence technologies using the OpenAI API are used to handle user enquiries.

When responses are generated using artificial intelligence, the content of the relevant requests is transmitted through the OpenAI API for processing and response generation.

Users can also contact support directly through Telegram.

Information processing by the Telegram platform itself is governed by that service's terms and privacy policy.

Information protection

Organisational and technical measures are applied when processing the information received to prevent unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Personal data enquiries

Requests for information about personal data processing, correction, restriction of processing or deletion are considered in accordance with applicable law.
