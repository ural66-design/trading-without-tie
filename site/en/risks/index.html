Risk warning

General provisions

Trading financial instruments, copying trades and using trading robots involve the risk of losing some or all of your funds.

Past trading results, monitoring figures and trading algorithm test results do not guarantee similar financial results in the future.

Trade copying risks

Copied trading results may differ from those of the source trading account due to differences in execution conditions, capital amounts, copying settings and other circumstances.

Algorithmic trading risks

Trading robots operate according to their programmed algorithms and may make both profitable and loss-making trades.

The effectiveness of trading algorithms depends on market conditions, configuration parameters, the characteristics of the trading platform used and the quality of trade execution.

Additional risk factors

Using leverage increases both potential profit and possible losses.

Financial market volatility, insufficient liquidity, slippage, technical failures and order execution delays may significantly affect trading results.

Independent decision-making

Decisions to register with a broker, choose a trading platform, trading strategy and a specific participant whose trades are to be copied, connect to trade copying services, use trading robots, and determine trading parameters and the amount of money involved are made by the user independently, at their own discretion, based on their own assessment of the relevant services' terms of use and the associated financial risks.
