Frequently asked questions

What is the copying multiplier? Why should it be set to 1?▶

The copying multiplier is a coefficient used to calculate the volume of copied trades, taking into account the ratio between the trader's (leader's) deposit and your account deposit.

Set the multiplier to 1 (one) when connecting. This avoids having to calculate the coefficient yourself and risking a settings error.

After the leader's trade closes, the enabled automatic scaling function will recalculate copying volumes as provided for by the broker.

It is therefore important to set the multiplier to 1 and enable “Scale after the leader closes their trades”.

What should I do if the email with my account details has not arrived?▶

Check your Spam folder and other email folders.

If you cannot find the email, contact the broker's support team to recover or clarify your account access details.

If necessary, you can ask for help contacting the broker's support team.

Where can I follow trading results?▶

You can follow trading in your Markets4you broker account.

You can also use MetaTrader 4 by connecting to the trading account with the investor password.

The Markets4you mobile app is additionally available in some countries.

What alternative funding methods are available?▶

Available funding methods depend on your country and the settings of your broker account.

In addition to bank cards, electronic wallets and other methods, including cryptocurrency, may be available.

If the method you need is not shown, complete verification. Additional funding options may become available afterwards.

Why is copying not working?▶

Trading does not take place around the clock, but when the necessary market conditions and trading signals are present. Sometimes several days or weeks may pass between trades. There is also no trading on weekends.

To make sure copying is connected correctly, sign in to your Markets4you broker account.

In the side menu, open “Copy trading” → “Copying rules”.

The list should contain the copying rule you created. By default, the broker names rules Rule 1, Rule 2, Rule 3 and so on.

If you have several rules, make sure the relevant rule relates to the T-Capital leader, if this information is displayed in your account.

If the rule is missing, repeat the connection procedure following the guide.

If the rule still does not appear after reconnecting, contact technical support at support@vladimir1.com or the Markets4you broker's support team.

What is an “investor password”?▶

An investor password is a special password for viewing a trading account in MetaTrader 4.

It allows you to observe open and closed trades, the balance and trading results.

It does not allow you to place trades yourself or withdraw money from the account.

Can I withdraw funds at any time?▶

You can submit a withdrawal request around the clock.

If your identity has not been verified before, the broker may require verification before the first withdrawal. Usually, you do not need to repeat it for every withdrawal.

With copying active, a normal withdrawal requires you to disable copying first, then reconnect after withdrawing the funds.

The exception is the broker's commission charge days, which occur twice a month. On those days, withdrawals may be made without disabling copying.

Request processing times depend on the broker and the withdrawal method selected.

What is leverage and what account type is needed for copying?▶

Copying uses a Classic Pro account with leverage of 1:2000.

Leverage determines the ratio between the size of a trading position and the required collateral.

High leverage allows less money to be used as margin for a position of a given size.

For the same position size, a price change produces the same financial result regardless of the leverage selected.

However, leverage does not eliminate market risks or the risk of insufficient margin.
