Frequently asked questions

About the project▶
What is Fast Money Bot?▶

Fast Money Bot (FMB) is a private project created by its author out of a personal interest in algorithmic trading, rather than for commercial purposes.

It serves as a showcase for the author's achievements in algorithmic trading, presenting original developments and their results under real market conditions.

Anyone who shares that interest can take part in testing the trading robot by automatically copying its trades.

How is Fast Money Bot different from other projects?▶

The fundamental difference between Fast Money Bot and conventional commercial services lies in its philosophy:

  • The project continues to exist and develop regardless of how many people join.
  • The author trades primarily for their own benefit.
  • Showing the developments publicly is intended to inform, not to advertise.
  • People interested in joining take the initiative themselves.
  • The author's fee is a by-product of the activity, not the project's main purpose.
How are trades copied?▶

Trades are copied through Markets4you's Share4you system.

The robot trades on the author's own account. The system automatically mirrors those trades on your account based on your trading capital and the copy multiplier you select.

This lets you test the author's algorithm in practice and assess its results yourself.

What are the conditions for joining FMB?▶

You can join freely and there is no subscription fee.

The arrangement is simple: if the author's system generates a positive financial result, the author receives 30% of new profits.

The broker settles payments automatically using a High-Water Mark. Recovering previous trading losses does not trigger another fee.

How much do I need to start trading?▶

The minimum trading deposit is $100.

The author strongly recommends holding additional reserve capital. With the standard copy multiplier of 1, the recommended reserve is 100% of the trading deposit. In exceptionally rare cases, the amount needed may be slightly higher.

If you change the copy multiplier, the recommended reserve changes in direct proportion. For example, with a $100 trading deposit:

  • Multiplier 0.5 — $50 reserve.
  • Multiplier 1 — $100 reserve.
  • Multiplier 2 — $200 reserve.

If reserve capital is already in use on the robot's trading account when you join, the minimum deposit for new participants increases by the amount in use. For example, if $100 in reserves has been added to the robot's $100 base deposit, the minimum for joining during that period is $200.

Holding your own reserve capital is strongly recommended by the author and is optional. You decide whether to set it aside and when to use it.

Where will my money be held, and who manages it?▶

All funds stay in your personal accounts with Markets4you. You do not need to send the author any money.

Through the copy system, the algorithm only carries out trading operations: opening, managing and closing positions.

You remain responsible for managing your capital, choosing your trading exposure, controlling risk and using your reserve funds when needed.

The author has no access to your personal trading accounts or funds.

Do I need to trade on my own?▶

No. The broker copies all trades automatically.

You do not need to analyse the market yourself, open or close trades, install the trading robot or leave a computer running.

How does the FMB trading robot work?▶

The Fast Money Bot trading robot works automatically. It opens, manages and closes positions according to the author's algorithm.

The system combines three trading baskets — trend-following, countertrend and compensation — and has eight proprietary functions.

It trades exclusively in the EUR/USD currency pair.

The algorithm's results are public and you can review them yourself.

Can I work directly with the project's author?▶

The author of Fast Money Bot is open to interesting proposals in algorithmic trading and practical applications of their work.

For individual collaboration, contact the author directly at support@vladimir1.com.

Getting connected▶
How do I connect to Fast Money Bot?▶

Complete four steps: register with Markets4you; complete verification; enable important Fast Money Bot notifications; and set up copying rules, with the account created automatically when you connect, then fund it using a convenient method. Detailed step-by-step instructions are available under “Connect” on the website and in the project’s Telegram bot.

Can I use my existing Markets4you account?▶

Yes. If you already have a Markets4you client area, you do not need to register again. Sign in to your existing account and follow the connection instructions.

Can I connect through a different broker?▶

The standard Fast Money Bot setup uses Markets4you and the Share4you copying system. Using another broker may be considered as part of an individual arrangement with the project’s creator. Terms are agreed individually.

What type of trading account does Fast Money Bot use?▶

Trades are copied to a Cent Pro cent account with leverage of 1:2000. The required account type is created automatically when you enable copying. A cent account lets you start trading with a $100 deposit, equivalent to 10,000 cents in the trading account.

Can I connect multiple trading accounts?▶

Yes. In the same Markets4you client area, you can connect several trading accounts with individual deposit amounts and copying multipliers. A separate copying rule is created for each account. The recommended reserve capital is also calculated separately for each account.

Do I have to connect to the Fast Money Bot Telegram bot?▶

The bot’s main function is to notify you promptly when reserve capital is needed during trading. These notifications are especially important when using aggressive settings for the trading algorithm. If Telegram is unavailable in your country, you can arrange another way to receive important notifications by emailing support@vladimir1.com.

When does trading start after I connect?▶

Trades are copied automatically once you create a copying rule and fund your trading account. New trades may not appear immediately. The trading robot opens positions only when market conditions meet the criteria set by its algorithm. Trading runs around the clock on weekdays (24/5).

How can I check that I connected correctly?▶

In the Markets4you client area, go to “Copy Trading” → “Copying Rules”. The copying rule you created should appear as “Rule №…”. Check that it is active, linked to the Fast Money Bot leader and connected to the correct trading account.

Who should I contact if I have trouble connecting?▶

Contact Markets4you support for help with registration, funding your trading account or technical issues with the copying system. For questions directly related to Fast Money Bot, email support@vladimir1.com.

Verification▶
What is verification and why is it necessary?▶

Verification is the process by which Markets4you confirms a client’s identity and residential address in line with applicable regulatory requirements. The broker handles the process directly. The creator of Fast Money Bot does not review documents or make verification decisions.

What documents are needed for verification?▶

You need two documents: an identity document with a photo, surname, given name and patronymic where applicable, such as a passport, ID card, residence permit or proof of permanent residence status; and proof of residence showing your surname and address. Submit unedited images of both documents. The broker may ask for additional documents or information in some cases.

Which documents can I use as proof of residence?▶

You can use the passport page showing your registered address, a utility bill, an internet or telephone bill, a bank statement, a residential lease or a similar document. The document must show both your surname and residential address.

What if my utility bills are in someone else’s name?▶

You can provide a residential lease or another similar document that confirms your residential address.

What if I live at a different address from the one registered in my passport?▶

For verification, give accurate information about where you live and provide documents to support it. The broker determines which forms of proof it accepts.

What if my country is missing from the registration list?▶

Choose the country closest to your country of residence so you can complete the registration form and proceed. During verification, submit documents that accurately show your country of residence. Under the FMB connection procedure, a broker representative will manually update the country to match your documents.

How long does verification take?▶

Review time depends on your country of residence, the documents submitted, the verification team’s workload and whether additional checks are needed. Fast Money Bot cannot influence verification times.

What should I do if the broker rejects my documents?▶

Check the reason in the broker’s client area and address the issues listed. Common reasons include poor image quality, missing information, discrepancies in personal details or an expired document. If you are unsure why your documents were rejected, contact Markets4you support.

Do I need to send my documents to the creator of Fast Money Bot?▶

No. Submit all documents exclusively through the official Markets4you interface. The project’s creator does not ask for identity documents, client area passwords or verification codes.

Who can help me with verification?▶

Contact Markets4you support directly for questions about document requirements and checks. If you need help communicating with the broker, send a request to an operator in the technical support section of the Fast Money Bot Telegram bot.
